### 1. Purpose of Data Processing (የውሂብ አያያዝ ዓላማ)
- **English:** The Wholesaler authorizes Birana to process credit sales invoices, customer retailer contact details, and bank/mobile money deposit statement exports solely for the purpose of accounts receivable reconciliation, payment verification, and daily settlement reporting via Daily Close.
- **Amharic:** ተሳታፊው የጅምላ ነጋዴ የብድር ሽያጭ ደረሰኞችን፣ የደንበኛ ቸርቻሪዎችን አድራሻ እና የባንክ/ቴሌብር ገቢ ሂሳብ መግለጫዎችን በዕለታዊ ሂሳብ ማጠቃለያ (Daily Close) መተግበሪያ አማካኝነት የሂሳብ ማስታረቅ፣ የክፍያ ማረጋገጫ እና የዕለታዊ ማጠቃለያ ሪፖርት ለማዘጋጀት ብቻ ውሂቡን እንዲጠቀም ለቢራና ቴክኖሎጂ ፈቃድ ሰጥቷል።

### 2. Proprietary Ownership & Data Sovereignty (የባለቤትነት መብት እና የውሂብ ሉዓላዊነት)
- **English:** All customer lists, credit balances, sales records, and financial statements remain the exclusive proprietary property of the Wholesaler. Birana shall never sell, monetize, disclose, or transfer any merchant data to third parties, credit bureaus, or advertising networks. All data is hosted exclusively on sovereign Tier III infrastructure within Ethiopia (Rule 7).
- **Amharic:** ሁሉም የደንበኞች ዝርዝር፣ ያልተከፈለ የብድር ቀሪ፣ የሽያጭ መረጃ እና የባንክ መግለጫዎች የጅምላ ነጋዴው ብቸኛ ንብረት ሆነው ይቀጥላሉ። ቢራና የነጋዴውን ውሂብ ለማንኛውም ሶስተኛ ወገን፣ የብድር መረጃ ቢሮ ወይም ለማስታወቂያ አያስተላልፍም አይሸጥምም። ሁሉም መረጃዎች በኢትዮጵያ ውስጥ ባሉ ደረጃቸውን የጠበቁ ዳታ ማዕከላት (Rule 7) ብቻ ይቀመጣሉ።

### 3. Read-Only Banking Statement Ingestion (የባንክ መግለጫ አያያዝ ወሰን)
- **English:** Statement ingestion is strictly read-only and restricted to inward credit deposits. Daily Close never requests, stores, or utilizes banking transaction PINs, online banking withdrawal passwords, or payment authorization tokens.
- **Amharic:** የባንክ እና የቴሌብር መግለጫዎች አጠቃቀም የገቢ ሂሳቦችን (ክሬዲት) ለማንበብ ብቻ የተገደበ ነው። መተግበሪያው የባንክ ሚስጥር ቁጥር (PIN)፣ ገንዘብ የማስተላለፍ ወይም የማውጣት የይለፍ ቃል አይጠይቅም፣ አያስቀምጥምም።

### 4. Right to Termination & Data Purge (ስምምነትን የማቋረጥ እና ውሂብን የመሰረዝ መብት)
- **English:** The Wholesaler may terminate pilot participation at any time upon written notice. Upon termination, Birana shall deliver a full database export to the Wholesaler and cryptographically purge all tenant records from active storage within seventy-two (72) hours.
- **Amharic:** የጅምላ ነጋዴው በማንኛውም ጊዜ የሙከራ ተሳትፎውን በጽሁፍ ማቋረጥ ይችላል። ስምምነቱ ሲቋረጥ ቢራና ሙሉ የውሂብ ኮፒ ለነጋዴው የሚያስረክብ ሲሆን በ72 ሰዓታት ውስጥ ሁሉንም የነጋዴውን መረጃዎች ከዋናው ሰርቨር ላይ ሙሉ በሙሉ ያጠፋል።

## Part 2: Retailer Customer Balance Privacy Notice  
### ለችርቻሮ ነጋዴ ደንበኞች የሚሰጥ የሂሳብ ሚስጥራዊነት ማስታወቂያ

*This notice is presented to retail shop owners via SMS, Telegram, and the secure balance verification link (`/confirm-balance/{token}`).*

### English Notice
> **Notice to Retail Partner:**  
> Your wholesale supplier uses **Daily Close** (powered by Birana Technology, Addis Ababa) to maintain accurate credit sales records and ensure your payments are properly credited to your account.
> 
> - **Information Handled:** We record your business trade name, store phone number, open delivery invoices, and payment confirmation receipts.
> - **Confidentiality Commitment:** Your outstanding credit balance is strictly confidential between you and your wholesale supplier. It is never shared with other merchants, competitors, or third-party marketers.
> - **Verification Security:** Balance confirmation links use secure, temporary cryptographic tokens. You are never asked for banking passwords, mobile money PINs, or cash deposits through this link.
> - **Questions or Corrections:** If you notice an error in your recorded balance or invoice details, contact your wholesale supplier's credit manager immediately.

---

> **ለተከበሩ የችርቻሮ ንግድ አጋራችን የተሰጠ ማስታወቂያ፡**
